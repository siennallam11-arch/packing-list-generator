# TripCover

The top of every trip page: a painted cover band, a round sticker badge with the suitcase doodle, the trip title in lowercase handwriting, and the dates.

**Provide:** the cover colour (strawberry by default), the cities joined with " + ", and the overall dates with nights and days.

- The paint texture is an SVG filter on a plain rect: never a photo.
- Title in `hand-display`, lowercase: "tokyo + kyoto". Meta in `type-meta`: "Dec 10 – Dec 15 · 5 nights · 6 days".
