# CityWeather

One row per city under the trip title: a weather doodle, the city in handwriting, its dates and temperature range, and a short weather blurb.

**Provide:** city, dates, low–high in °F, and the blurb text from the weather service.

- Doodle by weather: umbrella for rain, snowflake for cold or snow, sun for warm.
- Temperatures always in Fahrenheit: "36–55°F".
- The blurb is in `hand-note`, `pen`. Forecasts read "Chilly, with highs around…"; trips beyond two weeks say "Last year around these dates it was…" so it's clear it's not a forecast.
