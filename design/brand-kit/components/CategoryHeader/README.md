# CategoryHeader

The heading for each category: a toggle triangle, the category doodle, a handwritten "The …" title with a scribbled underline, and a count.

**Provide:** the category, its doodle, packed/total counts and `aria-expanded`.

- Titles always read "The documents", "The clothes", "The toiletries", "The tech", "The activity gear", "The odds & ends".
- When everything in it is packed, the title and underline turn `matcha-ink`, the count reads "9/9 packed", and the category folds itself.
